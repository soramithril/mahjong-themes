Terms of use

Mahjong Clubhouse · Last updated October 2026

Mahjong Clubhouse is a simple tool for a group of friends to plan game nights and keep score. By joining a club, adding your name, or signing in, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Be decent to each other

Everything you type in the app (your name, a club's name, a note on a night) is seen by the other people in that club. There is no tolerance here for content that is abusive, hateful, threatening, sexually explicit, or that harasses anyone, and none for abusive behaviour towards other players.

  • The app refuses some words outright.
  • Anyone can report a player from that player's page in the Players tab. We read every report and act on it, normally within 24 hours.
  • A club's organizer can take anyone off the club's list at any time.
  • We can remove content, remove someone from a club, or close an account or a club that breaks these terms, without notice.

Your club, your records

The scores, answers and nights in a club belong to that club. Anyone in the club can add nights, answer and keep score; organizers look after the list of players and the settings. Keep your invite code to the people you want in your club: anyone who has it can join.

Accounts

Members don't need an account. Organizers sign in with a code sent to their email, and are responsible for what is done with their account. You can delete your account at any time under Settings → Account. How we handle your information is in the privacy policy.

Pro and themes

The app is free. Some things in it can be bought through Apple's App Store:

  • Mahjong Clubhouse Pro is a yearly subscription. It starts with a free week. After that it costs the price shown in the app (in the United States, $9.99 a year), taken from your Apple account, and it renews by itself each year until you turn it off. Turn it off at least a day before the year ends, in your iPhone's Settings: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Turning it off during the free week costs nothing.
  • Pro lets you start as many clubs as you like, shows every tip for the table in full, has no ads, and gives you one premium theme free each season. A free theme you have picked stays yours after Pro ends.
  • Themes and theme packs are bought once and are yours to keep, on any iPhone signed in to the same Apple account. Use Restore purchases in Settings on a new phone.
  • Payments, refunds and family sharing are handled by Apple under its own terms. We never see your card.

No gambling

The app records games played and games won. It does not handle money, and is not for organizing gambling where that is against the law.

The app as it is

We work to keep the app running and your records safe, but it is provided as it is, without guarantees. We are not liable for lost data, for a night that went unscheduled, or for anything that happens at a game night. If something is wrong, tell us and we will try to put it right.

Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before the change takes effect.
