Privacy policy

Mahjong Clubhouse · Last updated October 2026

Mahjong Clubhouse helps a regular games club plan its nights and keep score. This page explains what the app keeps, who can see it, and how to remove it. The short version: we keep only what the app needs to work, we show it only to the people in your club, and we never sell it or use it for advertising.

What the app keeps

  • Your name, as you or your organizer typed it, in each club you belong to.
  • Your answers to each game night (in, maybe, out) and your scores: games played and games won.
  • How you signed in, for organizers. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we keep the ID they give us for you and, if you share it, your name and email address. With Apple you can choose to hide your email. If you sign in with an emailed code, we keep your email address and use it only to send those codes. We never see or keep a password. Members who just tap their name don't sign in at all.
  • An address for your phone, only if you turn on reminders. Apple gives it to us so we can send you notifications: a night added, a reminder to answer, and when scores are in. Turn reminders off in your phone's Settings and we stop using it.
  • What you have bought, if you buy Pro or a theme: the App Store's number for the purchase, what it was, and when Pro runs to. We check it with Apple, and keep it so the app knows what is yours. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card or your Apple account.
  • A sign-in token stored on your phone, so the app remembers which clubs you joined and which name is yours. It's essential for the app to work; it is not used to track you.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information with anyone for marketing.
  • No home addresses or phone numbers. The app does not ask for them or keep them.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, photos or anything else on your phone.

Who can see it

Everything above is visible only to people in the same club: names, answers and scores. Someone in a different club cannot see any of it. An organizer's email address is never shown to other members. If something is reported, the person who runs the app can see the report, the names involved and the club's name.

Where it's kept

The app runs on Cloudflare, which stores the data on our behalf. Sign-in codes are emailed through Resend. Both act only on our instructions and only to run the app. Data is held in North America. Notifications are delivered through Apple. When you sign in with Apple or Google, they confirm to us who you are; we don't send them anything about what you do in the app.

Removing your information

  • If you signed in: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This removes your account, email and name straight away. Your past scores stay in each club's history under "Former player", so the other players' standings still add up.
  • If you only tapped your name: ask your organizer to take you off the list, or email us and we'll remove your details.
  • Leaving a club on your phone (Settings → Your clubs) removes it from your phone only.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email the address below and we'll answer within 30 days. The person responsible for privacy at Mahjong Clubhouse is the app's owner, reached at the same address.

We rely on your agreement to keep this information, which you give by joining a club or signing in, and you can withdraw it at any time by leaving or deleting your account. If you are unhappy with how we've handled something, you can also complain to your privacy regulator: in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly keep their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
