Help

Mahjong Clubhouse

Get in touch

Email mahjongclubhousesupport@gmail.com and a person will answer, usually within a day or two.

Joining a club

Your organizer sends you a link or an eight-character code. Tap the link, or open the app and type the code, then tap your name on the list. You stay signed in on that phone.

Starting a club

Open the app, choose Start a new club, and sign in. Give the club a name, then send the invite to everyone from the home screen.

Picked the wrong name?

Settings → I'm not … takes you back to the list of names.

Putting in scores

After a night, tap the big button at the top of the home screen. Each player has two numbers: games played and games won. Tap + as you go, or all at once at the end.

Something someone wrote is not okay

Open Players, tap the person, and choose Report. Reports are read within a day. Your organizer can also take anyone off the list.

Removing your information

If you signed in: Settings → Account → Delete my account. If you only tapped your name, ask your organizer to take you off the list, or email us. There is more in the privacy policy.
